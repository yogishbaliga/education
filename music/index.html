How to use and keyboard shortcuts
Place
Pick a note or rest, then click a staff. Or drag it from the palette onto the staff.
Edit
Click a note to select it. Drag it up or down to change pitch. Use the bar at the bottom to change it.
A – G
Type a note after the selected one (nearest octave)
R
Add a rest after the selected note
1 – 5
Choose whole, half, quarter, eighth or sixteenth for new notes
.
Turn the dot on or off for new notes
↑ ↓
Move the selected note by step (hold Shift for an octave)
← →
Select the previous or next note
+ − N
Sharp, flat or natural on the selected note
Delete
Delete the selected note
Space
Play or stop (starts from the selected note’s measure)
Esc
Deselect, then switch to Select mode
